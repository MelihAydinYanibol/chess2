Rules
  • Click one of your pieces. It gets a random move type (pawn, rook, knight, bishop, queen or king).
  • Each piece gets one roll per turn. If it can't move, pick another piece. Once a piece can move, you must move it.
  • Check uses each piece's real type, as in normal chess.
  • Each time a king is checked it loses one move type at random. It never loses "king".
  • Checkmate: in check with no escape under any roll your pieces have left.
  • When a pawn reaches the far side, pick a move type: it's added to your roll list again, doubling its chance for all your pieces except the king. Each pawn does this once.
